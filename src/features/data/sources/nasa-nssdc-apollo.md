# NASA NSSDCA Apollo Landing Site Coordinates

- URL: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/lunar_sites.html
- Product: Apollo Landing Site Coordinates page, Lunar Module (LM) rows for missions 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17
- Provider: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, National Space Science Data Center (NSSDC/NSSDCA); curator David R. Williams
- Accessed: 2026-11-14
- Coordinate frame: planetocentric Mean Earth/Polar Axis Lunar Reference System, DE421 ephemeris; page states coordinates determined from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter imagery (Wagner et al., Icarus 283, 92–103 (2017)). The degree precision in the page is retained as published; do not infer a more accurate realization.
- Usage: NASA website content; no explicit license stated on the source page. Coordinates are factual values transcribed with attribution; verify applicable NASA media/website terms before redistributing page text or imagery.
- Included evidence (lat N, lon E): Apollo 11 LM 0.67416, 23.47314; Apollo 12 LM -3.0128, -23.4219; Apollo 14 LM -3.64589, -17.47194; Apollo 15 LM 26.13239, 3.63330; Apollo 16 LM -8.9734, 15.5011; Apollo 17 LM 20.1911, 30.7723.
- Limitations: Coordinates locate the LM in the named lunar frame; they do not supply local terrain/horizon shape, a complete uncertainty model, or elevation. Catalog retains `surfaceElevationM: null` for all Apollo sites.
