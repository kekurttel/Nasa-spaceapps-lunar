# Lunar polar evidence and PSR validation

This manifest points to NASA/PDS source records for later comparison. It is metadata only: **no masks or pixel assets were downloaded, no pixel agreement was measured, and this work makes no scientific claim about any pixel or location**. It does not label a point permanently shadowed. Permanent darkness requires temporal illumination support over the relevant solar/seasonal cycles; a cold thermal observation alone is not direct evidence of permanent darkness, and a LAMP UV albedo is not a direct full-time-shadow measurement.

## Verified source records

- **Thermal observation — LRO Diviner PCP**. [PDS Geosciences Node Diviner mission page](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/lro/diviner.htm) and [PDS4 PCP directory](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/lro/urn-nasa-pds-lro_diviner_derived1/data_derived_pcp/) were inspected. Mission-page coverage is 2009-07-05–2020-03-15. A collection XML or product label was not inspected, so exact product ID, map bins, resolution, coordinate convention, and spatial coverage remain unverified. PCP is thermal measurement, not a direct sunlight/shadow record.
- **Ultraviolet observation — LRO LAMP GDR**, product `LRO-L-LAMP-5-GDR-V1.0`. [PDS archive profile](https://pds.nasa.gov/ds-view/pds/viewProfile.jsp?dsid=LRO-L-LAMP-5-GDR-V1.0). The inspected profile describes calibrated night-side photon counts and derived brightness/albedo/illumination maps, cumulative and monthly, polar stereographic to ±80° and 240 m/pixel at the pole. Profile observation coverage is 2009-09-16T12:00:00Z–2010-09-14T12:00:00Z; map NoData is NaN. The exact DE/frame realization remains unknown until the individual product label is inspected. Albedo is illumination-model-dependent, including IPM Ly-alpha, stellar light, and LOLA topography; albedo differences alone do not demonstrate permanent shadow. Supporting published interpretation: [Gladstone et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1029/2011JE003913), which reports FUV/albedo observations in PSRs and discusses possible frost/porosity interpretations.
- **LOLA-derived illumination / PSR model**. [NASA GSFC PGDA Lunar Polar Illumination product page](https://pgda.gsfc.nasa.gov/products/69) lists `LPSR_65S_240M_201608`, `LPSR_75S_120M_201608`, and `LPSR_85S_060M_201608` shadow maps (plus north-pole products). It describes illumination modeled over hourly steps spanning an approximately 18.6-year lunar nutation cycle; 85°–90° coverage is 60 m/pixel, 75°–90° is 120 m/pixel, and 65°–90° is 240 m/pixel. The page identifies polar stereographic projection with 1737.4 km reference radius and true scale at pole; exact frame convention/epoch must be checked in product labels. This is a model, not an observation. The page requests citation of Mazarico et al. (2011), [DOI 10.1016/j.icarus.2010.10.030](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2010.10.030). Filename suffix `201608` is not interpreted as an epoch here. Diviner thermal and LAMP illumination/albedo products can share LOLA terrain inputs; they do not constitute independent terrain confirmation.

Primary source content was inspected directly, not just search snippets. The PGDA product page requests citation of Mazarico et al. for illumination products. [NASA/PDS citation guidance](https://pds.nasa.gov/datastandards/citing/) is recorded as citation guidance only, not a license or reuse-terms determination; product-specific copyright/reuse terms remain unverified. Manifest entries mark unknown frame details explicitly.

## Handoff / limits

A downstream comparison must obtain the exact intended archive products, inspect their labels for specific map/frame/version/time coverage, define the project mask and coordinate transformation, then report spatial/temporal sampling and uncertainty before calculating any overlap. This change performs none of those steps. Coverage fields and `unknown` values are descriptive metadata, not evidence that a region is in shadow. Validation checks manifest structure and required evidence-family presence only; it does not validate scientific correctness, product bytes, coordinates, or permanent-shadow status.

Validation entry point: `src/features/data/polar/evidence.mjs` exports deep-frozen `polarEvidenceSources` and `psrValidationOptions`, plus pure `validatePolarEvidence(sources, options)`. Tests: `tests/polar-evidence.test.mjs`.
